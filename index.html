Weekly Knee Rehab

Glutes • hamstrings • outer hip • knee control

Best time to do rehab

Main strength/landing sessions are best when you feel fresh and can control your knee well — usually late afternoon or early evening. Avoid hard landing/jump work when you are already fatigued.

If morning is your only option, warm up for 5–10 minutes first. Try to finish harder sessions at least 1–2 hours before bed if they make you feel too alert or sore.

Daily desk habit

Every 45–60 minutes, stand for 2–3 minutes.

• 10–20 steps • 5 hip extensions each side • 5 gentle squats • 5 glute squeezes
Stop and get reassessed if you develop:

painful clicking, catching/locking, new swelling, sharp joint-line pain, the knee giving way, or difficulty fully bending/straightening the knee.
